**7.4 Excluded Damages**: Consultant not liable for indirect, consequential, special, or punitive damages.

**7.5 Property Damage**: Consultant not responsible for:
- Damage caused by movers or other vendors
- Pre-existing damage to items or property

- Force majeure events (weather, natural disasters, etc.)

**7.6 Lost Items**: While Consultant will implement inventory systems, Consultant is not liable for items lost during move. Client should:

**7.8 New Home Condition**: Consultant not responsible for issues with new property (repairs needed, cleanliness, condition) unless specifically agreed in writing.
