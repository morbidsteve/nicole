- Legal advice

**12.2 Liability Cap**: Consultant's total liability shall not exceed total fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

**12.3 Property Damage**: Consultant not liable for:
- Acts of nature, weather events
- Gradual deterioration or maintenance issues
- Vendor negligence or failures
- Pre-existing conditions
- Issues not discoverable through visual inspection
- Failures of property systems or appliances

**12.4 Force Majeure**: Not liable for inability to access property due to weather, natural disasters, or government restrictions.

**12.5 Vendor Issues**: Not liable for quality, timeliness, or costs of third-party vendor services.

**12.6 Security**: While Consultant will follow security protocols, not liable for break-ins or theft.

**12.7 Insurance Requirement**: Client must maintain appropriate property and liability insurance. Consultant's services do not replace insurance.
