## 🎯 Which Contract to Use When

### Decision Tree

**Is this a one-time project?**
- **Relocation?** → Use Relocation Services Agreement
- **Other project (organization, event, etc.)?** → Use Hourly Services Agreement

**Is this ongoing service?**
- **20+ hours/month?** → Use Monthly Retainer Agreement
- **Seasonal/multiple properties?** → Use Snow Bird Services Agreement
- **Less than 20 hours/month?** → Use Hourly Services Agreement

**Does client require extra confidentiality?**
- **Executive, high-profile, or privacy-sensitive?** → Use Master Services Agreement + specific Service Order
- **Regular confidentiality sufficient?** → Use standard service-specific agreement

### Combining Contracts

**Option 1: Layer Approach**
- Sign Master Services Agreement & NDA first (establishes confidentiality and general terms)
- Add specific Service Orders for each project (pricing, scope, timeline)
- Best for clients using multiple service types over time

**Option 2: Standalone**
- Use single, service-specific agreement
- Good for most clients
- Simpler administration

## 💼 Contract Administration Best Practices

### Before Client Signs

1. **Provide contract in advance**: Give client time to review (at least 48 hours)
2. **Discuss key terms**: Don't assume they've read every section
3. **Answer questions**: Clarify anything unclear
4. **Be transparent**: Never hide terms or use contracts to trick clients
5. **Encourage attorney review**: Professional clients will appreciate this

### Signing Process

**Option 1: In-Person**
- Print two copies
- Both parties sign and initial each page
- Each party keeps original

**Option 2: Electronic**
- Use DocuSign, HelloSign, or similar
- Email PDFs for digital signatures
- Store securely
- Both parties receive copies

**Option 3: Email**
- Send PDF
- Client prints, signs, scans/photos, returns
- You sign, return final copy
- Less formal but legally binding

### After Signing

1. **Store securely**: Keep all signed contracts for minimum 7 years
2. **Create client file**: Include contract, invoices, project notes
3. **Add to calendar**: Set reminders for renewal dates, review dates
4. **Insurance**: Provide copy to insurance company if required
5. **Reference**: Refer back to contract when issues arise

## 🔒 Confidentiality & Data Protection

### Storing Client Information

**Physical Documents**:
- Locked file cabinet
- Secure location
- Shred when disposing

**Digital Files**:
- Password-protected computer
- Encrypted cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox Business)
- Never on unsecured devices
- Regular backups

**Access Credentials** (keys, codes):
- Secure lockbox or safe
- Never written with client name attached
- Return upon project completion or termination

### What NEVER to Do

❌ Discuss clients by name with others
❌ Post about clients on social media
❌ Share before/after photos without written permission
❌ Tell friends/family about "interesting" clients
❌ Leave client documents visible in your car
❌ Discuss client details in public places
❌ Save client info on unsecured devices

## 📊 Payment Processing

### Methods to Accept
1. **Bank Transfer/ACH**: Lowest fees, best for retainers
2. **Check**: Traditional, good for older clients
3. **Venmo/Zelle**: Quick, easy, no fees
4. **Credit Card**: Convenient but 2.9-3.5% fees (pass to client or absorb)
5. **PayPal**: Widely accepted, 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction

### Payment Terms
- **Deposits**: 50% upfront for projects over $1,000
- **Retainers**: Due 1st of month, auto-pay ideal
- **Project**: Net 15 (due within 15 days)
- **Late fees**: 1.5% per month after 15 days

### Invoicing Software
- **FreshBooks**: Small business favorite
- **QuickBooks**: More comprehensive
- **Wave**: Free option
- **Square Invoices**: Simple, integrates with payments

## ⚖️ Insurance Requirements

### What You Need
1. **General Liability Insurance**: $1-2 million coverage
   - Protects against property damage, injury
   - Required by many high-end buildings
   - Cost: $400-$800/year

2. **Professional Liability (E&O)**: $1 million coverage
   - Protects against claims of negligence, errors
   - Important for consulting/advice
   - Cost: $500-$1,200/year

3. **Bonding**: Optional but valuable
   - Protects client against theft
   - Important for clients with valuables
   - Increases trust with high-end clients
   - Cost: $150-$300/year

### Certificate of Insurance
- Keep digital copy ready to send to clients
- Update annually when renewing
- Required by many buildings and corporate clients

## 🚨 When Things Go Wrong

### Client Won't Pay
1. Send friendly reminder at 7 days
2. Formal notice at 15 days
3. Late fee starts accruing
4. Stop services at 30 days
5. Send to collections or small claims court at 60 days

### Contract Dispute
1. Review contract together
2. Attempt good faith resolution
3. Offer mediation
4. Consult your attorney
5. Litigation (last resort)

### Scope Creep
- Client wants more than contract includes
- Solution: Written change order with additional fee
- Never provide extra services without written agreement

### Confidentiality Breach (by you)
- Immediately notify client
- Apologize sincerely
- Consult attorney
- Review insurance
- Implement additional safeguards
