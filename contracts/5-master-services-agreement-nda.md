## 3. COMPREHENSIVE NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

### **3.1 RECOGNITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

Client acknowledges that Consultant's services require access to highly sensitive and private information, including but not limited to:

**Property Information**:
- Home addresses and locations
- Property layout, floor plans, and configurations
- Security systems, alarm codes, and access methods
- Safe combinations and key locations
- Surveillance system details
- Entry codes, garage openers, and access credentials
- Building access procedures for high-rise residences

**Personal and Financial Information**:
- Contents of home, including valuables and collections
- Personal belongings, wardrobe, and possessions
- Financial documents (inadvertently observed)
- Family information and relationships
- Lifestyle patterns and preferences
- Travel schedules and absences from home
- Health-related accommodations or equipment

**Professional and Business Information**:
- Client's professional role or business
- Work-related materials in home office
- Business contacts or documents
- Corporate relocation details

**Private Matters**:
- Personal habits and preferences
- Household dynamics
- Any sensitive personal information observed or learned

### **3.2 CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS**

Consultant agrees to:

**A. Complete Confidentiality**:
- Treat ALL information about Client as strictly confidential
- Never disclose Client's identity, name, address, or any identifying information
- Never discuss Client's affairs, home, or personal information with any third party
- Maintain confidentiality even in casual conversations
- Never reference Client by name or identifiable details in any context

**B. No Photography or Recording**:
- Not photograph Client's home, belongings, or property without explicit written permission
- Not make audio or video recordings
- Not allow others to photograph or record
- Use work documentation photos only with Client's written consent
- Never use any images for marketing, social media, or portfolio without separate written authorization (which Client may withhold)

**C. Information Security**:
- Store all Client information (addresses, codes, keys) securely
- Use password protection for any digital Client records
- Never share access codes or keys with anyone
- Destroy or return all Client information upon termination

**D. Third-Party Interactions**:
- When coordinating with vendors, disclose only minimum necessary information
- Not identify Client by name to vendors unless specifically authorized
- Require confidentiality from any subcontractors (with Client approval)
- Never gossip or discuss Client with vendors, colleagues, or others

**E. Social Media and Marketing Prohibition**:
- Never mention, tag, or reference Client on social media
- Never check in at Client's location on any platform
- Never share Client's address, neighborhood, or identifying details online
- Never post about work performed for Client without written permission

### **3.3 PERMITTED DISCLOSURES**

Confidentiality does not apply to:
- Information already in public domain through no fault of Consultant
- Information required by law, court order, or subpoena (with advance notice to Client if legally possible)
- Client's address for tax and business record-keeping only (kept confidential)
- Information needed for professional liability insurance (addresses only, no details)

### **3.4 SPECIAL CONFIDENTIALITY FOR EXECUTIVES & HIGH-PROFILE CLIENTS**

For clients in executive, professional, or high-profile positions, Consultant specifically agrees to:
- Never disclose association with Client
- Not acknowledge knowing Client if encountered publicly (unless Client initiates)
- Understand that discretion is paramount to Client's security and privacy
- Take extra precautions with all information
- Not disclose Client's professional role, employer, or industry

### **3.5 DURATION OF CONFIDENTIALITY**

All confidentiality obligations:
- Begin immediately upon signing this Agreement
- Continue during all service periods
- **Survive indefinitely after termination of services**
- Apply to all employees, contractors, or agents of Consultant

### **3.6 RETURN OF INFORMATION**

Upon Client's request or termination of services:
- Consultant will return all keys, access devices, and Client property
- Consultant will delete or destroy all Client information not required for legal/tax record-keeping
- Consultant will provide written confirmation of information destruction if requested
